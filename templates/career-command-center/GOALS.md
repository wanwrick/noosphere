## Decision Principles

When choosing between options, filter through:
1. Does this move me toward my target role and seniority?
2. Does this company/role match my industry and geography targets?
3. Does the comp meet my walk-away number?
4. Does the culture match how I work best?
5. Does this role open the next door — or close it?
