## Stage Definitions
- **Research** — identifying but haven't applied
- **Applied** — submitted, waiting to hear
- **Phone Screen** — recruiter or coordinator call scheduled/done
- **Hiring Manager** — first real interview
- **Panel / Loop** — multi-interview stage
- **Final Round** — last stage before decision
- **Offer** — offer received (see compensation-strategy.md for negotiation)
- **Closed - Rejected** — did not progress
- **Closed - Withdrew** — withdrew application
- **Closed - Accepted** — offer accepted

## Notes
- Flag any application that has been silent for 7+ days — either follow up or close it.
- One follow-up email is appropriate. Two is the maximum. Then close and move on.
- Volume matters early. Quality matters at interview stage.
