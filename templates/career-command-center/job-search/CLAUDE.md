# Job Search Domain

This folder tracks your active job search pipeline. Always read `../profile.yml` before giving advice in this domain.

## Files
- `applications-tracker.md` — live pipeline of every application, its stage, and next action
- `target-companies.md` — research brief per company you are seriously pursuing
- `ats-keywords.md` — keyword bank by role family for ATS optimization

## Routing
- To add or update an application → `applications-tracker.md`
- To research a specific company → `target-companies.md`
- To identify keywords for a role → `ats-keywords.md`
- To tailor resume for a posting → run `/tailor-resume`
- To write a cover letter → run `/write-cover-letter`
