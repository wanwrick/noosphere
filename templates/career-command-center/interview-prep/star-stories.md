# STAR Story Bank

Your most reusable interview asset. Build 8–10 stories and you are prepared for almost any behavioural interview.

**Formula:** Situation (brief context) → Task (your specific responsibility) → Action (what YOU did — not the team) → Result (quantified outcome)

**Time guideline:** 90–120 seconds when spoken aloud. Action should be 60% of the answer.

---

## Story Template — Copy and fill

### Story [#]: [Short title you'll remember, e.g., "The failed launch comeback"]

**Competencies this covers:** [e.g., resilience, stakeholder management, problem-solving]

**Situation (2–3 sentences):**
[Set the context briefly. What was happening? What was at stake?]

**Task (1–2 sentences):**
[What were YOU specifically responsible for?]

**Action (4–6 sentences — this is the bulk):**
[What did YOU do? Step by step. No "we." What decisions did you make? What did you say to whom? What did you choose NOT to do?]

**Result (2–3 sentences):**
[What was the measurable outcome? Use numbers. Then state the broader impact if relevant.]

**Versatility — this story answers:**
- Tell me about a time you failed.
- Describe a situation where you had to recover a project.
- Give me an example of handling pressure.
