# Behavioural Question Bank

Organized by competency. Each question maps to one or more STAR stories from `star-stories.md`.

For each question: note which story you'll use in the "Story #" column.

---

| Question | Story # |
|---|---|

## Motivation & Fit
| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| Why are you leaving your current role? | Prepare: honest, forward-looking, not bitter |
| Why do you want to work here specifically? | Prepare: specific and researched |
| Where do you see yourself in 5 years? | Prepare: ambitious but grounded in this role |
| What do you do when you disagree with your manager? | Prepare: constructive, not compliant |
| What are you looking for in your next role? | Prepare: aligned to what this role offers |

## Questions TO ASK
Always prepare 3–5 questions. Weak questions signal disinterest or lack of preparation.

**Strong questions:**
- "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?"
- "What's the biggest challenge facing the team right now that I'd be walking into?"
- "How does this team define and measure impact?"
- "What are the qualities of the people who have been most successful in this role?"
- "What do you wish you'd known before joining this company?"
