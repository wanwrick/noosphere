# Interview Prep Domain

This folder holds your interview preparation materials. Always read `../profile.yml` before giving advice here.

## Files
- `star-stories.md` — your reusable story bank (the most valuable file in this folder)
- `behavioral-questions.md` — curated question bank by competency
- `interview-retro.md` — post-interview debrief template

## Routing
- To prep for a specific upcoming interview → run `/prep-interview`
- To build or add a new STAR story → `star-stories.md`
- To browse questions by competency → `behavioral-questions.md`
- After an interview (within 24 hours) → `interview-retro.md`

## Key principle
Your STAR stories are assets. The same story (well-told) can answer many different questions. Build 8–10 strong stories and you are prepared for almost any behavioural interview.
