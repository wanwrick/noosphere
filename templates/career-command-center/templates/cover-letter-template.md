# Cover Letter Template

Use this file to store master cover letter drafts. Run `/write-cover-letter` to generate a tailored letter for a specific posting. Tailored versions are appended below.

---

## The 4-Paragraph Formula

Every cover letter produced by this system follows this structure. Max 350 words.

**P1 — Why this role:**  
Show you understand what the role is really for. Not "I am excited to apply." Something that signals you read between the lines of the job description.

**P2 — Why this company:**  
One or two specific, researched reasons. Not "I admire your culture." Something real — a product decision, a recent pivot, a stated value you can name and connect to your background.

**P3 — Why me:**  
Lead with your strongest relevant accomplishment (quantified). Follow with 1–2 qualifications that address the must-haves. Keep this factual and specific.

**P4 — Call to action:**  
One sentence. Invite next steps without begging.

---

## Master Opening Lines Bank

Avoid: "I am writing to express my interest in the [Title] position at [Company]."  
Use instead (customize to the role):

- "The [Title] role at [Company] is ultimately about [real challenge] — and that's the problem I've been solving for [X] years."
- "When [Company] [specific recent move or product decision], it signalled something I've been watching for: [what it meant to you]."
- "[Title] roles that actually succeed at [Company type] require [specific skill or judgment]. It's what I've built my career on."
