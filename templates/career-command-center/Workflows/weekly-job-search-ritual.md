# Weekly Job Search Ritual

A structured cadence to keep momentum without burning out. Job searching is a part-time job — treat it like one.

---

## Monday: Set the Week

**Time required:** 30 minutes

1. Open `job-search/applications-tracker.md`
2. Review what's in flight — what moved last week, what's gone silent, what needs a follow-up
3. Identify this week's 3 priority actions (apply to X, follow up with Y, fix Z in resume)
4. Set a daily application target (realistic: 2–5 tailored applications per week beats 20 mass applies)

**Ask yourself:** "What is the one thing, if I do it this week, that moves my search forward most?"

---

## Tuesday–Thursday: Execute

**Daily habit (30–60 minutes):**

- **Applications:** Tailor and submit. Use `/tailor-resume` + `/write-cover-letter` per posting.
- **Outreach:** 1–2 targeted LinkedIn messages or emails. Quality beats volume.
- **Prep:** If an interview is coming, start `/prep-interview` 48–72 hours out.
- **Research:** Add a new company to `job-search/target-companies.md` if you find a strong lead.

**Cold message formula:**
> "[Name], I noticed [specific thing about their work or company]. I'm a [your title] with experience in [relevant area]. I'd love to ask you [specific question about their experience]. Would you be open to a 20-minute call?"

No pitch. No resume attached. Ask for a conversation, not a favour.

---

## Friday: Weekly Review

Run `/weekly-review` skill.

Takes 15 minutes. Covers: pipeline status, win of the week, blocker, 3 next-week actions.

---

## Monthly: Strategy Reset

On the last Friday of each month:

1. Review pipeline conversion rates — applications → screens → interviews → offers
2. If screen-to-interview conversion is low: resume or LinkedIn problem
3. If application-to-screen conversion is low: targeting or ATS problem
4. If interview-to-offer conversion is low: interview skills or fit problem
5. Adjust strategy accordingly — do not keep doing what is not working

---

## Rules
- **No spray-and-pray.** Tailored applications only. 10 targeted beats 50 generic.
- **Follow up once, maximum twice.** Then close and move on.
- **Every "no" is data.** Ask for feedback when possible. Log it.
- **Protect your energy.** Batch rejections — do not check email obsessively.
- **Tell people.** Your next role will likely come through a warm introduction, not a cold apply.
