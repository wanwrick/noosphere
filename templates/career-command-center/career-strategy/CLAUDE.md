# Career Strategy Domain

This folder holds the strategic layer of your job search — positioning, compensation, and what happens after you land. Always read `../profile.yml` before giving advice here.

## Files
- `positioning-narrative.md` — your "who I am" story, used everywhere
- `compensation-strategy.md` — salary research, offer evaluation, negotiation tracker
- `90-day-plan.md` — template for after you accept an offer

## Routing
- To clarify or sharpen your professional narrative → `positioning-narrative.md`
- To research salary, evaluate an offer, or prepare to negotiate → `compensation-strategy.md`
- After accepting an offer and preparing to start → `90-day-plan.md`

## Key principle
Most people skip strategy and go straight to tactics (send resume, apply online). Strategy first: who are you, what problem do you solve, for whom, and why now. The clearer this is, the shorter the search.
