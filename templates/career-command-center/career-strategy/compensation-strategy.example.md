## Negotiation Playbook

### Principles
1. Never give a number first. Deflect: "I'm focused on fit right now — what is the budgeted range for this role?"
2. Always negotiate. Even a soft ask gets results: "Is there any flexibility on the base?"
3. Negotiate the full package, not just base. Equity vesting, signing bonus, extra vacation, professional development budget are all negotiable.
4. Counter in writing. It creates a record and gives them time to check with decision-makers.
5. Get the offer in writing before you resign anywhere.

### Counter-offer script
"Thank you for the offer — I'm genuinely excited about this role. Based on my research and the scope of the role, I was expecting something closer to [your target]. Is there flexibility to get to [specific number]?"

### When they say the budget is fixed
"I understand. Could we explore [signing bonus / extra week of vacation / earlier review date / professional development budget] instead?"

---

## Post-Offer Checklist
- [ ] Get full written offer with all components listed
- [ ] Verify benefits details (health, dental, RRSP match, vacation)
- [ ] Clarify bonus structure (target %, discretionary vs performance-based, when paid)
- [ ] Clarify equity terms (vesting schedule, cliff, strike price if options)
- [ ] Confirm start date and notice period alignment
- [ ] Resign professionally — do not burn a bridge
