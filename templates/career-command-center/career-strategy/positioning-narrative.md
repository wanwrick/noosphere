## "Tell Me About Yourself" Script (2-minute spoken version)

Structure:
1. **Opening hook** (15 seconds): One sentence that names your identity and the type of value you create.
2. **Career arc** (45 seconds): The 2–3 roles or pivots that made you who you are. Focus on the thread, not every job.
3. **Recent highlight** (30 seconds): One specific, quantified thing from your recent work.
4. **The bridge** (30 seconds): Why you are here, why now, what you are looking for.
