# 90-Day Plan Template

Use this after accepting an offer. Starting strong in a new role is as important as landing it.

The 90-day plan signal: 1) you are serious, 2) you think strategically, and 3) you will not need hand-holding. Optionally share a version of this with your hiring manager in your final conversation before day one.

## Days 1–30: Learn Before You Lead

**Objective:** Build context. Do not change anything. Do not have opinions yet. Ask questions.

**Priority actions:**
- [ ] Meet every direct team member 1:1 in first 2 weeks
- [ ] Map the key stakeholders — who does this role serve, who does it depend on?
- [ ] Understand the top 3 business priorities your team owns
- [ ] Identify where the pain is — what's broken, slow, or frustrating?
- [ ] Find the informal power map — who actually gets things done here?
- [ ] Learn the tools, processes, and cadences
- [ ] Deliver one small, visible win (even if minor — it signals competence)

**Key questions to answer by day 30:**
- What does "good" look like in this role according to my manager?
- What are the 2–3 things that would make my manager's life easier?
- Who are the most important internal relationships for me to build?

---

## Days 31–60: Build and Contribute

**Objective:** Start contributing meaningfully. Identify your first real project.

**Priority actions:**
- [ ] Identify the one project or initiative you will own in Q1
- [ ] Present a point of view on something (your analysis, your recommendation)
- [ ] Strengthen 3 key relationships beyond the immediate team
- [ ] Identify one process or tool gap you can improve
- [ ] Deliver against an agreed expectation from your manager

**Key questions to answer by day 60:**
- Am I building the right relationships?
- Am I solving the right problems?
- What feedback am I getting — stated and unstated?

---

## Days 61–90: Drive

**Objective:** Operate independently. Deliver measurable impact. Set your own agenda.

**Priority actions:**
- [ ] Complete a meaningful deliverable you own end-to-end
- [ ] Have a structured 90-day check-in with your manager
- [ ] Articulate your H2 priorities and get alignment
- [ ] Identify what you want to learn or develop in the first year
- [ ] Build one cross-functional relationship outside your immediate orbit

**Success metrics at day 90:**
- [What will you have delivered?]
- [What relationships will be established?]
- [What reputation are you building?]

---

## 90-Day Check-In Agenda (with manager)

1. Here is what I have learned and observed in the first 90 days [your synthesis]
2. Here is where I believe I have added value so far [specific examples]
3. Here is where I need more support or context [honest gaps]
4. Here are my priorities for the next quarter [your proposal]
5. Where do you see gaps in my priorities? Where would you redirect me?
