# Case Study Patterns Reference

## Platform/Marketplace Businesses

### Airbnb Pattern

**Problem**: Expensive hotels, underutilized residential space
**Solution**: Two-sided marketplace connecting hosts and guests
**Key Success Factors**:
- Solved chicken-and-egg by focusing on one city first
- Built trust through reviews, verification, insurance
- Leveraged existing platform (Craigslist) for initial supply
- Professional photography improved listing quality
- Handled payments to remove friction

**Assessment Questions**:
- How will you solve the chicken-and-egg problem?
- What's your geographic launch strategy?
- How do you build trust between parties?
- What's your take rate and how does it affect adoption?

### Uber Pattern

**Problem**: Unreliable taxi service, cash-based transactions
**Solution**: On-demand transportation via mobile
**Key Success Factors**:
- Started with premium segment (black cars)
- GPS tracking added transparency
- Rating system created accountability
- Surge pricing balanced supply/demand
- Expanded to adjacent markets (Uber Eats)

**Assessment Questions**:
- What existing behavior are you replacing?
- How do you balance supply and demand?
- What's the regulatory environment?
- Can you expand to adjacent use cases?

## Consumer Product Businesses

### DTC Brand Pattern (from course cases)

**Typical Characteristics**:
- Underserved segment in large market
- Premium positioning with direct relationship
- Storytelling/brand as differentiator
- Subscription or repeat purchase model
- Community building

**Key Success Factors**:
- Clear target customer persona
- Product-market fit before scaling
- Efficient customer acquisition channels
- High gross margins (>50%)
- Strong retention metrics

**Assessment Questions**:
- Who exactly is the target customer?
- What's the customer acquisition strategy?
- What's the LTV:CAC ratio?
- How defensible is the brand?
- What's the path to retail expansion?

### Ellie's Earth Pattern (Youth Natural Deodorant)

**Problem**: Youth lack access to premium natural deodorants designed for them
**Solution**: Youth-focused natural deodorant with cool branding
**Key Considerations**:
- Niche within niche (youth + natural + premium)
- Two customers: kids (users) and parents (buyers)
- Supply chain complexity (international tubes, domestic manufacturing)
- Brand must resonate with both demographics
- Distribution strategy: DTC → specialty retail → mass

**Scaling Challenges**:
- Home-based fulfillment won't scale
- Manufacturing partner relationship critical
- International supply chain risk
- Margin pressure in wholesale channel

## Food & Beverage Businesses

### Evandale Caviar Pattern

**Problem**: North American caviar market underserved by domestic production
**Solution**: Sustainable sturgeon farm in New Brunswick
**Key Considerations**:
- Long time to revenue (3+ years to first harvest)
- High capital requirements ($7M+)
- Regulatory complexity (aquaculture)
- Technical expertise required (fish farming)
- Market access through chef relationships

**Assessment Framework Application**:
- People: Do they know aquaculture?
- Opportunity: Is the market big enough?
- Context: Regulatory environment favorable?
- Risk: What if fish don't thrive? Market doesn't develop?

### Wife Wine Bar Pattern (Experiential Hospitality)

**Problem**: Lack of sophisticated, women-focused wine experiences
**Solution**: Premium wine bar with education and community focus
**Key Considerations**:
- Location critical (demographics, rent, accessibility)
- Multiple revenue streams (tastings, membership, retail, events)
- High fixed costs (rent, staff, inventory)
- Break-even timeline typically 18-24 months
- Scalability limited (physical expansion required)

**Key Metrics**:
- Revenue per square foot
- Table/seat turnover
- Average ticket
- Membership conversion and retention
- Event booking rate

## Technology/SaaS Patterns

### B2B SaaS Pattern

**Key Metrics to Assess**:
- MRR/ARR growth rate
- Net Revenue Retention (>100% = expansion)
- Gross Margin (>70% target)
- CAC payback (<12 months)
- Churn rate (<5% monthly)

**Assessment Questions**:
- Is this a vitamin or painkiller?
- What's the buying process?
- Who's the decision maker vs user?
- What's the implementation complexity?
- How sticky is the product?

### B2C Tech Pattern

**Key Metrics to Assess**:
- DAU/MAU ratio (>30% = good engagement)
- Viral coefficient
- User growth rate
- Monetization per user
- Retention curves

## Pattern Recognition Framework

When assessing any venture, identify which pattern it most resembles, then:

1. **Compare to successful examples**: What did winners in this pattern do right?
2. **Identify pattern-specific risks**: What kills businesses in this pattern?
3. **Assess deviation from pattern**: Is the deviation a feature or bug?
4. **Look for pattern evolution**: Is this an improvement on existing pattern?

## Common Failure Patterns

### Premature Scaling
- Spending on growth before product-market fit
- Hiring ahead of revenue
- Expanding geographically too fast

### Undifferentiated Entry
- Entering crowded market without clear advantage
- Competing on price in low-margin business
- "Me too" product without innovation

### Wrong Market Timing
- Technology not ready
- Customer behavior not evolved
- Regulatory barriers not addressed

### Team Gaps
- Technical founder without business skills
- Business founder without technical execution
- No one with industry expertise

### Capital Mismatch
- Business requires more capital than available
- Burn rate exceeds funding runway
- Unrealistic milestones for funding amount
