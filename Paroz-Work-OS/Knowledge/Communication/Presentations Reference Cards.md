🎤 PRESENTATION QUICK REFERENCE CARD
## Your At-a-Glance Executive Presentation Guide

---

## 🚀 RAMP OPENING (30 seconds)
```
R → "Good morning, it's great to be here with you today..."
A → [Story/Question/Statistic/Quote]
M → "Today I'll show you how to..."
P → "We'll cover 3 key points in the next 5 minutes..."
```

---

## 💬 INSTANT FRAMEWORKS

### BLUF (Answer First)
```
"We should do X."
"Here's why: [2-3 reasons]"
"Next step: [specific action]"
```

### PREP (Make Your Point)
```
P: "My point is..."
R: "Because..."
E: "For example..."
P: "Therefore..."
```

### SBI (Give Feedback)
```
S: "In Monday's meeting..."
B: "When you did X..."
I: "The impact was..."
```

## 💪 PRESENCE REMINDERS

### Stand Strong
- Feet shoulder-width
- Open stance
- Hands visible
- Shoulders back
- Chin level

### Speak Strong
- Bottom line first
- No hedging
- Pause for power
- Project voice
- End sentences down

### Think Strong
- Stay present
- Read the room
- Listen fully
- Stay calm
- Bridge to message

## ❓ Q&A POWER MOVES

### When You Know:
"Great question. [PAUSE] The answer is..."

### When You Don't:
"Important question. I'll get you that answer by [date]."

### When It's Hostile:
"I understand your concern. Let me address that..."

### When Off-Topic:
"Interesting point. Let's discuss offline. For now..."

### The Bridge:
"That relates to my earlier point about..."

---

## 📊 VISUAL RULES
- **3-Second Test**: Message clear at a glance?
- **One Idea**: Single concept per slide
- **More Pictures**: Visual > Text
- **White Space**: Don't fill every inch
- **Your Stage**: You're the star, not slides

---

## 🚫 NEVER SAY
- "I think..."
- "Sort of..."
- "Just..."
- "Maybe..."
- "I'm sorry, but..."
- "To be honest..."
- "I'm no expert..."
- "Does that make sense?"

## ✅ ALWAYS SAY
- "I recommend..."
- "The data shows..."
- "My experience indicates..."
- "Three key points..."
- "Here's what matters..."
- "The bottom line is..."
- "Let me be clear..."
- "The evidence suggests..."

---

## ⚡ EMERGENCY FIXES

### Lost Your Place:
"Let me return to the key point..."

### Technical Failure:
"While we fix this, let me tell you..."

### Time Running Out:
"In the interest of time, here's what matters most..."

### Blank Mind:
"The critical thing to remember is..." [return to Big Idea]

### Heckler/Interruption:
"I appreciate your passion. Let's continue and address that in Q&A."
