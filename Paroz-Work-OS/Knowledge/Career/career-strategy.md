## Salary Negotiation (BATNA from Negotiation SKILL)

| Element | How to Determine |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Your BATNA** | Best alternative if this negotiation fails (other offers, current role, consulting) |
| **Their BATNA** | Cost of not hiring you (re-run search, lost productivity, opportunity cost) |
| **ZOPA** | Range where both sides benefit |
| **Reservation price** | Walk-away number (minimum acceptable) |
| **Aspirational target** | Ideal outcome (anchor high, justify with evidence) |

### Negotiation Tactics
1. **Never give a number first.** "I'd like to understand the full scope before discussing compensation."
2. **Anchor with evidence.** Market data + specific achievement metrics
3. **Negotiate the package, not just salary.** Signing bonus, equity, remote, title, learning budget
4. **Use silence.** State your ask, then stop talking
5. **Get it in writing.** Verbal agreements are worthless

**Source:** `_Career/02-Interview-Prep/09_SALARY_NEGOTIATION.md`

## Offer Evaluation (10-10-10 from Critical Thinking)

| Factor | Weight | Assessment Criteria |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Base compensation | 25% | Market benchmark comparison |
| Total comp (bonus, equity, benefits) | 15% | Full package value |
| Role scope and title | 15% | Step-up vs. lateral |
| Growth trajectory | 15% | Where does this lead in 3-5 years? |
| Technology and problems | 10% | Interesting vs. boring work |
| Team and manager quality | 10% | Will you learn? |
| Culture and values fit | 5% | Congruence Model applied |
| Location and flexibility | 5% | Remote, hybrid, relocation |

### 10-10-10 Decision Framework
- **10 minutes:** How do I feel about this right now? (gut check)
- **10 months:** Will I have learned, grown, and built momentum?
- **10 years:** Does this move me toward my long-term career vision?
