## D1: Delegation — What Work Goes to AI?

| Sub-Skill | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| **Problem Awareness** | Understand your goals and work nature before involving AI |
| **Platform Awareness** | Know capabilities and limitations of different AI systems |
| **Task Delegation** | Distribute work to leverage strengths of humans AND AI |

---

## D2: Description — How to Communicate with AI

| Sub-Skill | Focus | Example |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Product Description** | Define output: format, audience, style | "Write a 500-word executive summary for the CFO" |
| **Process Description** | Define approach: step-by-step instructions | "First analyze the data, then identify trends, then recommend actions" |
| **Performance Description** | Define behavior: concise vs detailed, challenging vs supportive | "Be concise and challenge my assumptions" |

---

## D3: Discernment — How to Evaluate AI Output

| Sub-Skill | Question to Ask |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Product Discernment** | Is the output accurate, appropriate, coherent, relevant? |
| **Process Discernment** | Did the AI reason correctly? Any logical errors or lapses? |
| **Performance Discernment** | Is the AI's communication style effective for my needs? |

---

## D4: Diligence — How to Use AI Responsibly

| Sub-Skill | Principle |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Creation Diligence** | Be thoughtful about which AI systems you use and how |
| **Transparency Diligence** | Be honest about AI's role in your work |
| **Deployment Diligence** | Take responsibility for verifying and vouching for outputs you use/share |

## Human-AI Interaction Modes

| Mode | Description | Human Role |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Automation** | AI performs specific tasks based on specific instructions | Define what needs to be done |
| **Augmentation** | Human + AI collaborate as thinking partners | Iterative back-and-forth, both contribute |
| **Agency** | AI works independently on human's behalf | Establish knowledge & behavior patterns |

## Key AI Technical Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **LLM** | Large language model — trained on vast text data to understand/generate language |
| **Parameters** | Mathematical values (billions) determining how model processes information |
| **Transformer** | 2017 architecture enabling parallel text processing with attention mechanisms |
| **Context Window** | Max information AI considers at once (conversation + documents) |
| **Hallucination** | AI confidently states plausible but incorrect information |
| **Knowledge Cutoff** | Date after which AI has no built-in world knowledge |
| **Temperature** | Controls randomness: high = creative/varied, low = predictable/focused |
| **RAG** | Retrieval Augmented Generation — connects AI to external knowledge sources |
| **Fine-tuning** | Additional training after pre-training for helpfulness and safety |
| **Scaling Laws** | Empirical: larger models + more data = consistent performance improvements |

## Prompt Engineering Techniques

| Technique | What It Does | When to Use |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Chain-of-Thought** | Ask AI to work through problem step by step | Complex reasoning, multi-step problems |
| **Few-Shot (N-Shot)** | Show examples of desired input-output pattern | When format/style matters, hard to describe |
| **Role/Persona** | Specify character or expertise level | Domain-specific tasks, audience matching |
| **Output Constraints** | Specify format, length, structure | Consistent deliverables, integration needs |
| **Think-First** | Ask AI to reason before answering | Important decisions, nuanced analysis |

## Quick Decision Guide: When to Use AI

| Good for AI | Keep for Humans |
|-------------|-----------------|
| First drafts and brainstorming | Final judgment and decisions |
| Summarizing and synthesizing | Ethical/political sensitivity |
| Code generation and debugging | Relationship-dependent work |
| Research and exploration | Accountability-critical outputs |
| Format conversion and restructuring | Creative vision and strategy |
| Pattern recognition in data | Novel/unprecedented situations |
