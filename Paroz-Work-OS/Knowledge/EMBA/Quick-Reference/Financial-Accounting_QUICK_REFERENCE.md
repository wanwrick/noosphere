## Core Equations

```
Assets = Liabilities + Owners' Equity

Assets − Liabilities = Permanent OE + Revenue − Expenses + Gains − Losses

Cash = Liabilities − All Other Assets + Permanent OE + Net Income

ROE = (NI/Sales) × (Sales/Assets) × (Assets/OE)
    = Profitability × Efficiency × Leverage
```

## Key Ratios Cheat Sheet

| Ratio | Formula | Measures |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Gross Margin | (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue | Pricing power & cost control |
| Operating Margin | Operating Income / Revenue | Core business profitability |
| Net Profit Margin | NI / Revenue | Bottom-line profitability |
| Asset Turnover | Revenue / Avg Total Assets | Asset utilization efficiency |
| DSO | (A/R / Revenue) × 365 | Collection speed |
| Inventory Turnover | COGS / Avg Inventory | How fast inventory sells |
| Days in Inventory | 365 / Inventory Turnover | Days to sell inventory |
| Equity Multiplier | Assets / OE | Financial leverage |
| Debt/Equity | Total Liabilities / OE | Capital structure |
| Interest Coverage | EBIT / Interest Expense | Debt service ability |
| Free Cash Flow | CFO − CapEx | Cash available after maintenance |

## Revenue Recognition (ASC 606) — 5 Steps

1. Identify the **contract**
2. Identify **performance obligations**
3. Determine **transaction price**
4. **Allocate** price to obligations
5. **Recognize** revenue when obligation satisfied

## Cash Flow Adjustments (Indirect Method)

| Adjustment | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| Depreciation/Amortization | + Add back |
| Gains on asset sales | − Subtract |
| Losses on asset sales | + Add back |
| Increase in A/R | − Subtract |
| Decrease in A/R | + Add |
| Increase in Inventory | − Subtract |
| Decrease in Inventory | + Add |
| Increase in A/P | + Add |
| Decrease in A/P | − Subtract |
| Increase in Deferred Revenue | + Add |

## Inventory Method Comparison (Rising Prices)

| Method | COGS | NI | Ending Inventory | Taxes |
|--------|------|-----|-----------------|-------|
| FIFO | Lower | Higher | Higher | Higher |
| LIFO | Higher | Lower | Lower | Lower |
| Avg Cost | Middle | Middle | Middle | Middle |

**LIFO→FIFO conversion:** FIFO Inventory = LIFO Inventory + LIFO Reserve
