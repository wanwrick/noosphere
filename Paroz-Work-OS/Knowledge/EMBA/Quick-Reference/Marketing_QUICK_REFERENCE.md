## Thomas: 9 Customer Strategy Frameworks (Quick Pull)

|---|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | GO/STOP + ACE | Why customers buy or don't | GO signals (triggers) vs STOP signals (barriers); score on Attention–Coherence–Emotion |

| 4 | Positioning Statement | Your strategy in one sentence | "To [target], Brand X is [frame] that offers [distinctiveness]" |

## LaBarge: Strategic Marketing Frameworks

### Outside-In vs Inside-Out
| Inside-Out ❌ | Outside-In ✅ |
|---|---|
| "What else can we make?" | "What else can we do for customers?" |
| Product at the pinnacle | Customer needs at the pinnacle |
| Cost leadership, differentiation | Shape perceptions, accumulative advantage |

### Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) — Christensen
- Unit of analysis = **the job**, not the customer
- Jobs are functional + social + emotional
- Look for: non-consumption, workarounds, surprising uses
- Find jobs by: identifying problems → combining solutions → splitting solutions
- **Beware feature creep** — irrelevant to actual customer needs

### Customer Decision Journey (CDJ) — McKinsey
Not a funnel — it's circular and iterative:
1. Need Recognition → Knows Brand
2. Actively Considers → Searches → Evaluates
3. Willingness to Pay → Plans to Buy
4. Consumes → Satisfied
5. Loyal → Engages → Advocates

### Design Thinking — Stanford d.school / Brown
Five phases: **Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test**
- Human-centered, iterative, bias toward action, embraces ambiguity

### Experimentation — Thomke
- Goal: establish **causation** (not correlation)
- Types: A/B tests, field experiments, market tests
- Measure behavioral outcomes, not self-reports
- Build culture of experimentation — democratize testing

### ICE Learning Model
- **I**deas → **C**onnections → **E**xtensions (increasing depth)

## Key Formulas

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|---------|

| Break-Even Sales | Total Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin |

| K-Factor (Virality) | # invitations × conversion rate |
| MMM | Sales = α + β₁(Price) + β₂(Promo) + β₃(Dist) + β₄(Ads) |

## AI Marketing Strategy Checklist (Davenport et al.)
1. ☐ What **data inputs** required? (behavioral, metadata, product, historical)
2. ☐ What **model type**? (classification, clustering, generative, regression)
3. ☐ **Real-time or batch** deployment?
4. ☐ **Evaluation metrics** — managerial (CTR, conversion) + tech (accuracy, latency)
5. ☐ **Integration method** — ESP, CDP, dashboards?

## Paroz's Applied Context (Quick Summary)

**Dove Brand Strategy** (Thomas): 5-year strategy using GO/STOP + brand association mapping. Strengthen: empowerment, diversity, sustainability. Weaken: basic functional positioning.

**RBAC as Design Thinking + Experimentation** (LaBarge — Course Reflection):
- Empathy: 3 pain narratives; only 27% of 600+ AD groups served real security functions
- JTBD reframe: "What job are these groups doing?" → 73% = psychological comfort
- Finance pilot: hypothesized 60% admin reduction → achieved 65%
- Decision velocity 3.5× improvement, innovation cycle 45→12 days, 85% voluntary adoption
- Key insight: "Cognitive consonance" — Design Thinking builds empathy, Experimentation proves it

**Insurance Doc Automation** (LaBarge — Post-Class 3): Document creation 1hr→5min, accuracy 99.2%, CSAT +23pts. Speed wasn't primary value — real-time co-review transformed broker-client dynamic.

**Fieldcraft Coffee Critique** (LaBarge — Peer Critique): Applied Ries & Trout positioning theory + perceptual mapping to expose authenticity paradox in veteran→outdoor brand pivot. Recommended 2×2 segmentation (outdoor intensity × coffee expertise).
