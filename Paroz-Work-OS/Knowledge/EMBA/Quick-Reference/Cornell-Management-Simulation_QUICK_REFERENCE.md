## Unit Economics

```
Revenue:  Price per unit
- COGS:   Material + Labor + Depreciation + Reject rate
= GROSS PROFIT per unit

- OpEx:   (SG&A + Advertising + R&D + Process Improvement) / Total Units Sold
= CONTRIBUTION per unit

- Interest & Tax
= NET INCOME / Shares = EPS

Goal: Make EPS positive and growing → increases share price
```
