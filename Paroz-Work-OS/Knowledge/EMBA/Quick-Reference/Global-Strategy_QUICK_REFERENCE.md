## CAGE Distance Quick Guide

| Dimension | Key Question | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Cultural** | Do we share language, religion, values? | US → UK (low) vs US → Japan (high) |
| **Administrative** | Compatible legal/political systems? | EU single market (low) vs US → Cuba (high) |
| **Geographic** | How far? Time zones? Borders? | US → Canada (low) vs US → New Zealand (high) |
| **Economic** | Similar income/cost levels? | US → Germany (low) vs US → Bangladesh (high) |

## IJV Negotiation — 6 Decision Rules

1. What is best for the JV (not just one partner)?
2. What is the standard international practice?
3. Long-term approach or hedging?
4. What are the actual customer issues?
5. What are your indifference points?
6. Is your partner left room to profit?
