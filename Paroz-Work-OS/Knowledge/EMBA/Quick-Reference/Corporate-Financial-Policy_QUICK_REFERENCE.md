## Core Formulas

```
WACC = %Eq × ke + %Debt × kd(1−t) + %Pref × kp

V(levered) = V(unlevered) + t×D                           (with taxes only)
V(levered) = V(unlevered) + t×D − PV(CFD × π)             (Trade-Off Theory)

β(levered) = β(unlevered) × [1 + (1−t) × D/E]            Hamada's Equation
β(unlevered) = β(levered) / [1 + (1−t) × D/E]            Unlevering Beta

Cash Conversion Cycle = Days in A/R + Days in Inventory − Days in A/P
NLB = Cash + Marketable Securities − Notes Payable
```

## Capital Structure Theories

| Theory | Core Idea | Implication |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| M&M Irrelevance | Structure doesn't matter in perfect markets | Baseline — "slicing the pie" doesn't change its size |
| Tax Shield | V = Vu + tD | More debt → more value via tax savings |
| Trade-Off | Optimize tax shield vs distress costs | There IS an optimal leverage level |
| Pecking Order | Internal → Debt → Equity | Firms prefer cheapest/least-signal source |
| Risk-Shifting | Distressed firms gamble with bondholder money | Market prices this in → higher cost of debt |
| Debt Overhang | Existing debt blocks positive NPV investments | Under-investment problem |

## M&A Quick Framework

**5 Steps:** Strategy → Screening → Evaluation → Negotiation → Integration + Tracking

**Synergies:** Cost (more reliable) > Revenue (less reliable)

**Control Premium:** 25-40% above market price

**Valuation Methods:**
- Market-based: Comparable Analysis, Precedent Transactions
- Income-based: DCF (FCFF at WACC or FCFE at ke)
- Asset-based: Adjusted Book Value, Liquidation Value, Replacement Cost

**Integration Outcomes:** Autonomy | Semi-Autonomy | Absorption | Assimilation

## Dividends vs Share Repurchases

| | Dividends | Buybacks |
|--|-----------|----------|

| Signal | Ongoing confidence | Undervaluation signal |
| Rating view | Fixed charge | Discretionary |
