## The Big Picture

**Financial Accounting = RULES** (external, GAAP, historical)
**Managerial Accounting = TOOLS** (internal, flexible, future-oriented)

Three pillars: **Measurement → Decision Making → Control**

## Cost Classification Cheat Sheet

| Dimension 1 | Dimension 2 | Examples |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct + Variable** | Traced, changes with volume | Raw materials, piece-rate labor |
| **Direct + Fixed** | Traced, constant | Dedicated machine depreciation |
| **Indirect + Variable** | Allocated, changes with volume | Utilities, supplies |
| **Indirect + Fixed** | Allocated, constant | Plant rent, supervisor salary |

**Total Cost** = DM + DL + OH | **Prime** = DM + DL | **Conversion** = DL + OH

## Key Formulas

### Overhead Allocation
$$POHR = \frac{\text{Estimated Total OH}}{\text{Estimated Allocation Base}}$$

$$\text{Applied OH} = POHR \times \text{Actual Base in Cost Object}$$

### CVP Analysis
$$\text{BEP (units)} = \frac{FC}{CM_{unit}} \qquad \text{BEP (\$)} = \frac{FC}{CM\%}$$

$$\text{Target Profit (units)} = \frac{FC + \text{Target Profit}}{CM_{unit}}$$

$$CM\% = \frac{\text{Price} - \text{Variable Cost}}{\text{Price}}$$

### Operating Leverage
$$DOL = \frac{\text{Total CM}}{\text{EBIT}}$$

*DOL of 4 → 20% sales change = 80% EBIT change*

### Variance Analysis (Direct Costs)
$$\text{Price Variance} = AQ \times (AP - SP)$$
$$\text{Quantity Variance} = SP \times (AQ - SQ)$$
$$\text{Total Variance} = (AP \times AQ) - (SP \times SQ)$$

### Capital Budgeting
$$EVA = NOPAT - (Capital \times WACC)$$

NPV > 0 → Accept | IRR > Discount Rate → Accept

### Transfer Pricing
- **Min TP (seller)** = Incremental cost + opportunity cost of lost sales
- **Max TP (buyer)** = Lower of external price or internal value

## Flexible Budget Decomposition

```
Static Budget Variance
├── Sales Volume Variance (volume effect)
│   ├── Market Size
│   └── Market Share
└── Flexible Budget Variance (operational)
    ├── Selling Price
    ├── Direct Cost (Price + Quantity)
    ├── Variable OH (Spending + Efficiency)
    └── Fixed OH (Spending + Volume)
```

## Relevant Cost Decision Rules

| Decision | Rule | Watch For |
|----------|------|-----------|
| **Make vs Buy** | Compare incremental cost vs purchase price | Exclude allocated common FC |
| **Keep vs Drop** | Drop if CM < direct fixed costs saved | Substitution effects between lines |
| **Special Order** | Accept if price > incremental cost + capacity | Never include allocated FC |
| **Product Mix** | Maximize CM per unit of scarce resource | Rank by CM/constraint, not CM/unit |

**Always irrelevant:** Sunk costs, allocated common fixed costs, future costs that don't differ
