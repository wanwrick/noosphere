## Framework Cheat Sheet

### Courage Quotient
```
CQ = Willingness to Act / Fear
```
**Raise numerator:** Connect to values → Ripple Effect → Hidden Costs of Inaction → Strategic Timing
**Lower denominator:** See it → Own it → Use it

### Hidden Costs of Inaction (When YES = NO)
Saying YES to inaction = YES to comfort, resentment, lowered standards, never knowing potential
Saying YES to inaction = NO to getting what you want, living up to values, self-respect

### SPLIT Framework (Global Teams — Neeley)
| Dimension | Focus |
|---|---|
| **S**tructure | Team design, roles, reporting |
| **P**rocess | Workflows, decision-making, communication cadence |
| **L**anguage | Common language strategy, fluency support |
| **I**dentity | Shared team identity across cultures |
| **T**echnology | Tools that bridge geographic distance |

### Group Emotional Intelligence (Druskat & Wolff)
Three levels of norms: Individual member → Group → Cross-boundary
Build: Trust → Group Identity → Group Efficacy

### Human Capital vs. Social Capital
- **Human Capital** = How you stand out (skills, knowledge, character)
- **Social Capital** = How you are connected (ties, trust, benefits)
- **Social Capital lets you leverage Human Capital**

### Networking BCDs
- **B**readth & Diversity — avoid echo chambers
- **C**onnections — bridge across boundaries
- **D**ynamic — future-oriented, not static

### Focused Leader (Goleman)
Three types of focus: **Inner** (self-awareness) | **Other** (empathy) | **Outer** (systems)

### Case Analysis (4 Steps)
1. **Identify** issues, problems, opportunities
2. **Analyze** root causes (why, not what)
3. **Develop** & evaluate alternatives
4. **Recommend** action

| Intellectual Humility | Recognizing your beliefs might be wrong (82% think they're usually right) |
| Authenticity Paradox | Growth requires behaviors that initially feel inauthentic (Ibarra) |
