## DiSC Quick Guide

| Style | Focus | Motivated By | Under Stress |
|-------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **D** (Dominance) | Results, action | Control, achievement | Loses patience, becomes demanding |
| **i** (Influence) | Enthusiasm, people | Recognition, excitement | Becomes disorganized, overly optimistic |
| **S** (Steadiness) | Stability, support | Harmony, helping | Resists change, becomes passive |
| **C** (Conscientiousness) | Accuracy, quality | Expertise, correctness | Becomes overly critical, withdrawn |

## Conflict Resolution (CBT Framework)

```
1. RECOGNIZE → Strong emotion = signal to slow down
   Ask: Is this thought valid? Is there another way to see this?

2. REFRAME → Replace automatic thought with productive one
   "They have no idea" → "They see it from a different angle"

3. RESPOND → Choose proportional, constructive action
   From lashing out → assertively stating needs
```

## Social Contract Essentials

```
A social contract should cover:
  ☐ Shared values and mission
  ☐ Communication norms (response times, channels)
  ☐ Decision-making process
  ☐ Role expectations and rotations
  ☐ Conflict resolution procedures
  ☐ Accountability mechanisms
  ☐ Quality standards for deliverables
  ☐ Procedures for handling violations
```

## Self-Improvement Framework

```
When frustrated with a teammate:
  1. INTERNAL SELF-AWARENESS — What emotions/assumptions are driving MY reaction?
  2. EXTERNAL SELF-AWARENESS — How are MY words/actions impacting others?
  3. PERSONAL ACCOUNTABILITY — How am I contributing to this situation?
```

## Team Roles (Rotating)

| Role | Job |
|------|-----|
| **Lead** | Owns direction and quality |
| **Second** | Supports Lead, quality check |
| **Chair** | Runs meetings, manages time |
| **Scribe** | Documents decisions and action items |
| **Contributor** | Delivers individual portions on time |

## 360 Peer Review Categories

```
COMMUNICATE — Debate to solve, not to be right; treat conflict as learning
ADAPT       — Hold others accountable; offer help; contribute to debriefs
RELATE      — Respect all members; meet deadlines; deliver quality work
EDUCATE     — Give/receive feedback; acquire new skills; seek consensus
```
