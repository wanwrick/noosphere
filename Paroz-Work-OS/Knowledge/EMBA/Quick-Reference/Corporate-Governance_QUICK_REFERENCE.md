## 🎯 THE ONE QUESTION THAT MATTERS
**How do we ensure managers act in shareholders' best interests when ownership and control are separated?**

---

## 📚 CORE FRAMEWORKS (Know These Cold)

### Jensen's Value Maximization (2001)
- **Objective**: Maximize long-term firm value (debt + equity + all claims)
- **Key Rule**: Cannot maximize in multiple dimensions simultaneously
- **Enlightened Version**: Manage stakeholder relationships AS MEANS to value creation

### Wallace's Empirical Finding (2003)
- Profitability FIRST → Then stakeholder investment
- Value destroyers (bottom MVA quartile) MORE likely to overspend on stakeholders
- Value creation and stakeholder investment are COMPLEMENTARY

### Corporate Governance 2.0 (Subramanian, 2015)

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| 1. Long-term Authority | End earnings guidance; dismantlable staggers |
| 2. Best People | Meaningful 3rd-party evaluations; skills > boxes |
| 3. Orderly Voice | Reasonable shareholder process; advance notice pills |

## 🧮 KEY FORMULAS

| Metric | Formula | Use |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **EVA** | NOPAT - (Capital × WACC) | Value created above cost of capital |
| **ROC** | NOPAT / Invested Capital | Return generated on capital |
| **TSR** | (P₁ - P₀ + Div) / P₀ | Total shareholder return |
| **Relative TSR** | Company TSR vs. Peer TSR | Isolate management contribution |

**Value Creation Rule**: ROC > WACC = Value Created

## 🛡️ ANTI-TAKEOVER DEFENSES

| Defense | Effect | Gov 2.0 View |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| Poison Pill | Dilutes acquirer | "Advance notice" pill preferred |
| Staggered Board | 14+ month delay | Dismantlable version OK |
| Dual-Class | Permanent control | Generally disfavored |

**Maximum Entrenchment** = Dual-Class + Staggered Board + Poison Pill

## 🔍 CASE QUICK REFERENCE

### Tesla CEO Compensation
- **Issue**: $55B package, absolute metrics, captured board
- **Failures**: No profitability gates, Kimbal on comp committee, no relative TSR
- **Lesson**: Alignment without expertise = dangerous blind spots

### Shake Shack
- **Issue**: 790bp margin decline, 0% ROC, activist campaign
- **Structure**: Dual-class (58% Class B), staggered board
- **Lesson**: Valid charges can't overcome structural defenses

### SVB Failure
- **Issue**: Duration mismatch, liquidity crisis, 3 lines failed
- **Failures**: No CRO (9 months), risk committee lacked banking expertise
- **Lesson**: Expertise on board matters for specialized risks

## ⚠️ COMMON ANALYSIS MISTAKES

1. ❌ Accepting "irreplaceability" (= succession failure)
2. ❌ Confusing correlation with causation (past success ≠ validation)
3. ❌ Ignoring board conflicts (look for family, business ties)
4. ❌ Binary thinking (alternatives exist beyond approve/reject)
5. ❌ Market cap = value creation (could be multiple expansion)

---

## 📋 THREE LINES OF DEFENSE

| Line | Owner | Function |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1st | Management | Day-to-day risk ownership |
| 2nd | Risk/Compliance | Independent monitoring |
| 3rd | Internal Audit | Independent assurance |
| Board | Risk Committee | Oversight of all three |

## 💡 SECOND-ORDER INSIGHTS

> "Alignment without expertise creates dangerous blind spots" - Captured boards approve flawed plans

> "Irreplaceability isn't a feature, it's a governance failure" - Demands succession milestones

> "Governance theater vs. substance" - Internal evaluations ≠ meaningful evaluations

> "Multiple objectives = no objective" - Jensen's core critique of stakeholder theory
