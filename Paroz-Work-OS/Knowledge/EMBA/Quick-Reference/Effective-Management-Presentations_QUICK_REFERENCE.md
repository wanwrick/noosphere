# Effective Management Presentations - Quick Reference

## RAMP Opening Framework (30 seconds)
- **R - Rapport Builder:** Greet warmly, thank hosts/distinguished members
- **A - Attention Getter:** Provocative question, story, startling stats, quote, or demonstration
- **M - Message:** State purpose clearly + WIIFM (What's In It For Me)
- **P - Plan:** Preview points, state Q&A timing, indicate length

## Monroe's Motivated Sequence (Persuasive Speeches)
1. **Problem:** State problem, show impact on listener with examples
2. **Solution:** Explain how it works, cite experience, address objections
3. **Visualization:** Positive (if adopted) | Negative (if not) | Contrast (both)
4. **Action:** Be precise, specific, include timeline
- Wrap with RAMP-style close reinforcing WIIFM

## SUCCESs Principles Checklist
- [ ] **Simple:** One core message—distill ruthlessly
- [ ] **Unexpected:** Hook with curiosity or surprise
- [ ] **Concrete:** Specific examples, paint mental pictures
- [ ] **Credible:** Cite credible sources, provide evidence
- [ ] **Emotional:** Connect to what audiences care about
- [ ] **Stories:** Use narrative structure; 63% recall vs. 5% for data alone

## BLUF Principle (Bottom Line Up Front)
1. **Lead** with key message (decision, request, recommendation)
2. **Support** with 2-3 reasons/facts
3. **Offer** next steps/action needed
- Cuts fluff: Lead with conclusion, then justify

## PREP Framework (Impromptu Speaking)
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **P** | State main point upfront |
| **R** | Provide reason/rationale |
| **E** | Use specific example/data |
| **P** | Restate main point to reinforce |

## Executive Presence: Three Pillars

| Pillar | Key Traits |
|--------|-----------|
| **Gravitas** | Confidence, decisiveness, vision, integrity, respect |
| **Communication** | Superior speaking, command a room, authentic, reads audience |
| **Appearance** | Polished, fit, dresses appropriately, curates online image |

## Q&A Handling Strategy (10-Point System)
1. State rules upfront (when, how many questions)
2. Prepare for worst-case questions beforehand
3. Maintain control—formally recognize questioners
4. Include group—move away from questioner, paraphrase for everyone
5. Ask your question first to prime the pump
6. Admit you don't know (max 2x per session)
7. Leverage audience expertise when applicable
8. Acknowledge dissenters respectfully
9. Avoid "That's a good question" repetition
10. **End strong—never end on questions; always finish with closing statement**

## Opening Techniques
- **Rapport Builder:** Warmth, gratitude, connection
- **Attention Getter:** Problem hook, surprising stat, provocative question, compelling story
- **WIIFM:** Make clear what's in it for the audience
- **Preview:** State structure ("I'll cover 3 points...")

## Closing Techniques
- Summarize main points
- Quote someone relevant
- Tell a story
- Use poetic language
- Call to action
- Tie back to opening ("Recall when I mentioned...")
- Avoid new information; reinforce benefits

## Physical Presence Do's & Don'ts

**Do:**
- Carry yourself confidently
- Use open body language
- Make deliberate, purposeful movements
- Maintain calm, neutral expression
- Keep steady eye contact

**Don't:**
- Slouch or lean back
- Use closed gestures (crossed arms)
- Touch your face
- Avoid eye contact
- Make yourself small
- Nod excessively

## Vocal Presence Do's & Don'ts

**Avoid Weak Language:**
- ❌ Validation tags ("you know?" "don't you agree?")
- ❌ Qualifiers ("just," "a bit," "pretty much," "perhaps")
- ❌ Hedges ("I feel like," "I might be wrong, but...")
- ❌ Self-deprecation ("I'm no expert but...")

**Do:**
- Speak deliberately and with conviction
- Project voice without shouting
- Use pauses strategically
- Maintain calm, steady volume

## Presentation Structure (5-Minute Baseline)
- **Opening:** ~30 seconds (RAMP)
- **Body:** ~4 minutes (3 key messages × 3 supporting points each)
- **Close:** ~30 seconds (memorable, reinforce benefits)

## The Big Idea Formula
> "I believe [unique perspective] because [evidence], and if we don't act, [consequence], but if we do, [benefit]."
- Must be 1-2 sentences, memorable, repeatable
- Drives all content selection

## Stress Management Techniques
- Embrace nervousness as energy
- Practice until content is automatic
- Deep breathing exercises
- Visualization of success
- Physical activity beforehand
- Power poses (2 minutes expansive postures)
- Proper sleep, food, hydration

## Virtual/Webcam Fixes
- Position camera at eye level
- Professional, uncluttered background
- Proper lighting (face not backlit)
- Test audio; use quality microphone
- Minimize notifications
- Look at camera lens, not screen
- Increase energy by ~20%
- Use more facial expressions
- Stand if possible for important presentations
