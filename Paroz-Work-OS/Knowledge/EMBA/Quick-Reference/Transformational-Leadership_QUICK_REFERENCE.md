## The 4 I's of Transformational Leadership

```
1. IDEALIZED INFLUENCE    — Be the role model (ethical, principled, walk the talk)
2. INSPIRATIONAL MOTIVATION — Paint the compelling vision (energy, meaning, confidence)
3. INTELLECTUAL STIMULATION — Challenge the status quo (creativity, no criticism of ideas)
4. INDIVIDUALIZED CONSIDERATION — Coach each person (mentor, listen, delegate for growth)
```

---

## Transactional Leadership Quick Guide

```
Contingent Reward:
  → Set SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
  → Provide feedback (past performance) vs. advice (future behavior)
  → Recognize and reward achievement

Management-by-Exception:
  Active: Monitor and intervene proactively before problems escalate
  Passive: React only when problems are brought to your attention

  TIPP Skills: Timely | Immediate | Proportional | Private
```

## 9 Verbal Charismatic Leadership Tactics (CLTs)

```
1. Metaphors & analogies      — Make abstract concrete
2. Stories & anecdotes         — Create emotional connection
3. Moral conviction            — Express beliefs about right/wrong
4. Group sentiment reflection  — Articulate what audience feels
5. High expectations           — Set ambitious goals with confidence
6. Communicating confidence    — Express certainty goals are achievable
7. Three-part lists            — Group ideas in threes for rhythm
8. Rhetorical questions        — Engage thinking, no answer expected
9. Contrasts                   — Juxtapose ideas for clarity/emphasis
```

## 5 Non-Verbal CLTs

```
1. Animated voice    — Vary pitch, pace, volume, pauses
2. Facial expressions — Match the emotional content
3. Gestures          — Purposeful hand/body movements
4. Body posture      — Open, confident, lean toward audience
5. Eye contact       — Direct, sustained with individuals
```

## Dark Side of Leadership

```
Laissez-faire: Absence of leadership → confusion, conflict, low morale
Abusive supervision: Sustained hostile verbal/non-verbal behaviors
Pseudo-transformational: Appears transformational but serves own interests (narcissistic)
```
