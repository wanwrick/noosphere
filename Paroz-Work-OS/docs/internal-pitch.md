# Internal Pitch: EMBA Learning Operationalized

## Executive Summary

### Situation

Completed the Cornell-Queen's Executive MBA with 29 courses spanning 8 business domains: finance, strategy, marketing, leadership, operations, economics, technology, and governance. Daily work at Questrade requires applying these frameworks to data platform decisions, stakeholder communication, team leadership, and strategic planning.

### Complication

MBA knowledge has a well-documented decay problem. Frameworks learned in class stay in notebooks. Context switching between daily operational fires and strategic thinking makes consistent application difficult. The investment in executive education risks becoming a credential rather than a capability multiplier.

### Resolution

Built a structured AI knowledge system (Work OS) that integrates all 29 courses, Questrade work context, reusable templates, and step-by-step workflows into every working session. Claude acts as an AI partner that automatically loads priorities, applies named frameworks, and learns from corrections. The system is 29 files, ~4,350 lines, and requires zero external infrastructure.

## The System at a Glance

```
29 files, organized into four layers:

Core:       Routing brain + current priorities (P0-P3)
Knowledge:  29 EMBA courses (8 domains) + 5 work context files
Action:     4 templates + 4 workflows for real deliverables
System:     55 skills, 6 integrations, learning loop
```

**Key capabilities:**
- Automatically loads team context, priorities, and past corrections every session
- Routes questions to the correct MBA framework(s) by domain
- Produces deliverables using standardized templates (decision memos, status updates, RCAs, user stories)
- Integrates with Notion, Gmail, Google Calendar for live work context
- Self-improving: every correction is logged and never repeated

---

## ROI Story

**Speed:** Analysis and document creation time reduced significantly. A decision memo that previously took 2-3 hours now takes 30-45 minutes because the system applies frameworks automatically and uses pre-built templates.

**Consistency:** Every recommendation is grounded in a named framework. No more ad-hoc analysis. Stakeholders receive structured options (3 alternatives + recommendation) rather than informal proposals.

**Decision Quality:** Strategic decisions are evaluated through multiple EMBA lenses simultaneously: strategy + finance + operations + leadership implications. Cross-domain analysis that would take hours happens in minutes.

**Knowledge Retention:** The half-life problem is eliminated. All 29 courses are accessible in every working session. Frameworks are applied, not just remembered.

## What This Means for Questrade

1. **EMBA investment directly improves daily work output.** The knowledge is not theoretical; it produces better memos, better decisions, better stakeholder communication.

2. **Frameworks are operationalized, not memorized.** Porter's Five Forces, DCF analysis, Pyramid Principle, and dozens of other frameworks are applied consistently because the system routes to them automatically.

3. **The system is self-improving.** Every correction makes it smarter. Over time, the quality gap between ad-hoc work and system-assisted work widens.

4. **The approach is replicable.** Other leaders investing in executive education (or any structured learning) could build similar systems to operationalize their knowledge.
