# CDO Top 10 Deliverables

> **Source:** Synthesized from public CDO-discipline literature and prior practice. Origin-unclear in chain-of-custody — ships with explicit "as synthesized" disclaimer. Forks that identify a definitive primary source should add the citation.

## Core insight

A Chief Data Officer's portfolio aggregates into ten deliverables. Mapping any data initiative to one (or more) of these ten gives a CDO a single shared frame for prioritization and trade-offs.

## The 10 Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data Strategy & Roadmap** | Multi-year strategy; investment roadmap; vendor + platform choices |
| 2 | **Data Governance Framework** | Policies, roles, councils, decision rights |
| 3 | **Data Quality Management** | Quality standards, monitoring, remediation processes |
| 4 | **Master Data Management** | Single source of truth for entities (customer, product, employee) |
| 5 | **Metadata Management** | Lineage, glossary, technical + business metadata |
| 6 | **Data Architecture** | Reference architecture; lakehouse / warehouse / mesh patterns |
| 7 | **Data Literacy & Self-Serve** | Training, documentation, self-serve enablement |
| 8 | **Data Monetization** | Internal value capture + (where applicable) external monetization |
| 9 | **Privacy & Compliance** | Regulatory regimes (OSFI / PIPEDA / GDPR / HIPAA / SOC 2 / SOX) |
| 10 | **Data Culture & Change Management** | Adoption, behavior, learning systems |

## Strategic insight

- Prioritize gaps by **business impact**, not deliverable order.
- **Self-Serve and Culture** (#7 + #10) are typically the highest-leverage gaps in mature orgs — but they require Deliverables 2 + 5 + 6 to be in place first.
- Use this framework to **position the data platform as a strategic asset** to the C-suite, not a cost center.

## When to use this in this practice

- **CDO / VP-level conversations** — provides the shared vocabulary.
- **Initiative archetype mapping** — every active archetype should connect to ≥ 1 deliverable.
- **Annual data-strategy reviews** — the 10 deliverables become the slide outline.
