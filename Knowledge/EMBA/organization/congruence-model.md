# Congruence Model — Nadler & Tushman

> An organization performs well when its **four core elements** are aligned. Misalignment between any two elements produces friction, attrition, and missed strategy.

## The four elements

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Strategy** | Formal plan: Where to play, How to win, what to invest in |
| **People** | Skills, capabilities, mindset, motivation |
| **Work** | Tasks, processes, technology, workflows |
| **Structure** | Org chart, reporting lines, decision rights, formal authority |

## The congruence test

Each pair of elements must reinforce the other. Misalignment patterns:

| Misalignment | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Strategy vs People | Right strategy, wrong skills → frustration + attrition |
| Strategy vs Work | Right strategy, wrong processes → motion without progress |
| Strategy vs Structure | Right strategy, wrong reporting lines → ambiguous accountability |
| People vs Work | Skilled team, mismatched workflows → frustration |
| People vs Structure | Talent in wrong roles → underutilization |
| Work vs Structure | Process owns nothing; structure assigns nobody → chronic incidents |

## Diagnostic question

For any organizational dysfunction, ask: **which two elements are out of alignment?** The dysfunction will surface there, even if the symptom appears elsewhere.

## Common scenario in data platforms

- **Strategy:** "Become a producer-side platform team."
- **People:** Senior engineers who built the artifacts.
- **Work:** Maintaining template library, validation logic, governance bundle.
- **Structure:** ❌ No formal owner for Databricks application layer.

The first three are aligned; the fourth is the gap. The Congruence Model gives the executive language to name it: *Strategy, People, Work — congruent. Structure — gap.*

## When to use

- Diagnosing chronic team / org dysfunction.
- Slide 1 of a platform-team mandate proposal (the gap visualization).
- Post-reorg health check.
- Onboarding new leaders into a complex org.
