# Strategy Diamond — Hambrick & Fredrickson

> A complete strategy is *five* interconnected choices. Each must be internally consistent with the others. Changing one usually requires adjusting the rest.

## The five elements

| Element | Question | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Arenas** | Where will we be active? | Geography · product categories · channels · customer segments · vertical integration depth |
| **Vehicles** | How will we get there? | Internal R&D · acquisition · joint venture · licensing · alliance |
| **Differentiators** | How will we win? | Image · brand · cost · customization · product features · service · technology |
| **Staging** | What's our speed and sequence? | First-mover · fast-follower · phased rollout · big-bang launch |
| **Economic Logic** | How will we earn returns? | Premium pricing · scale economies · low-cost producer · network effects · proprietary platform |

## The internal-consistency test

The five elements must reinforce each other. Examples:

- ✅ Cost-leader Arena + Internal-R&D Vehicle + Cost Differentiator + Phased Staging + Scale-economies Economic Logic — coherent.
- ❌ Cost-leader Arena + Acquisition Vehicle + Differentiation-on-features + Big-bang Staging + Premium pricing — incoherent (cost-leadership is incompatible with differentiation-on-features).

If you can't articulate one element, the strategy is incomplete. If two elements contradict each other, the strategy will fail in execution.

## When to use

- Any "What is our strategy?" question — Hambrick forces a holistic, non-generic answer.
- M&A integration planning — assess whether the target's strategy diamond fits the acquirer's.
- Strategic-review preparation (board / steering committee).
- Case-interview answers — *"By the Strategy Diamond, this firm's strategy is to compete in [Arena] via [Vehicle], differentiating on [Differentiator], with [Staging] sequence, earning returns through [Economic Logic]."*

## Common gaps

- **Skipping Staging.** "We'll do everything by Year 3" is not a staging plan.
- **Generic Economic Logic.** "Drive shareholder value" is not an economic logic; "scale economies in cloud infrastructure" is.
- **Overemphasizing Differentiators.** Differentiators alone don't define a strategy; they have to be supported by the other four.
