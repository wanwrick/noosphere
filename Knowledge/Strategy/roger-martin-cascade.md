# Roger Martin's Strategic Choice Cascade

> Strategy is a cascading set of five choices. Each choice constrains the next. A strategy with gaps in any choice is incomplete.

## The five choices

```
+--------------------------+
| Winning Aspiration       |  ← What does winning look like?
+--------------------------+
            |
            v
+--------------------------+
| Where to Play            |  ← Which markets · segments · channels · geographies?
+--------------------------+
            |
            v
+--------------------------+
| How to Win               |  ← What's our distinctive value proposition?
+--------------------------+
            |
            v
+--------------------------+
| Capabilities             |  ← What must we be able to do?
+--------------------------+
            |
            v
+--------------------------+
| Management Systems       |  ← What systems support and reinforce the choices?
+--------------------------+
```

## The five questions

| # | Choice | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Winning Aspiration | What is the purpose of our enterprise; what does winning look like? |
| 2 | Where to Play | Which playing field will we choose to compete on? |
| 3 | How to Win | What is our distinctive way to win on that field? |
| 4 | Capabilities | What capabilities must we have to win? |
| 5 | Management Systems | What systems do we need to build and sustain those capabilities? |

## Internal consistency

Each downstream choice must be **derivable** from the upstream choices. If your "How to Win" doesn't follow from your "Where to Play," one or both is wrong.

## Common failures

| Failure | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Winning Aspiration is vague | "Be the best" — meaningless; no constraint on Where/How |
| Where to Play is too broad | "Everywhere our customers are" — no choice means no strategy |
| How to Win is undifferentiated | "Best service" — every competitor claims this |
| Capabilities don't follow from How to Win | We claim cost-leadership but invest in feature differentiation |
| Management Systems aren't redesigned | Strategy changes but compensation, hiring, processes don't |

## Applied: a data platform example

| Choice | Answer |
|---|---|
| Winning Aspiration | Become the trusted self-serve substrate for all analytical and AI work in [scope]. |
| Where to Play | Producer-side platform for the lakehouse layer; not consumer-side reporting. |
| How to Win | No LAC architecture (capability without bottleneck) + AI-Ready Platinum (1× publish → 5+ consume). |
| Capabilities | Metadata-driven ingestion · contract-driven validation · UC ABAC · MCP-enabled tool catalog. |
| Management Systems | DABs Golden Path · 10Q intake · governance audit · weekly producer ritual · sanitization protocol. |

## When to use

- Annual strategic-planning sessions.
- New venture / product-line launches.
- Major pivots.
- Any "what is our strategy?" conversation that needs structure.
