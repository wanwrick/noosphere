# Capability Matrix

Skill × level grid. Four levels: foundational · competent · proficient · authoring. "Authoring" means writes the practice IP, not just executes against it.

| Capability | Foundational | Competent | Proficient | Authoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data contract authoring | Reads a contract | Edits a contract for a known source | Authors a contract from a fuzzy intake | Designs the contract schema itself |
| DABs bundle rendering | Runs the bundle | Modifies a resource template | Adds a new render path | Owns the template repo |
| UC ABAC modeling | Reads tags | Adds a tag for a known tier | Designs the ABAC strategy for a domain | Writes the tier policy |
| DPIA + classification | Reads the runbook | Applies it to a known case | Authors a DPIA for a new producer | Owns the compliance register |
| AI consumption contracting | Reads a contract | Drafts one for a known agent | Authors one against the 5 Principles | Updates the principles |
| Producer-side intake (10Q) | Reads the 10Q | Runs an intake under supervision | Runs intake solo · drafts contract | Adapts the 10 questions for new domains |
| Platform mandate coalition | Recognizes the pattern | Briefs an adjacent leader | Runs the full coalition sequence | Authors the playbook |
| Defending AI spend | Reads the 12Q memo | Drafts the appendix | Authors the one-page memo | Picks the kill criterion |

## Reading the matrix

- **Bench depth** = count of "competent or higher" per row.
- **Authoring concentration** = count of capabilities the practice has at "authoring" level. A practice with zero authoring capabilities is a consumer of others' IP, not a creator of its own.
- **Hiring trigger** = any row with zero "proficient or higher" coverage. That is the next role brief.
- **Promotion trigger** = any individual at "proficient" with three observed authoring instances. They have started authoring; recognize it.
