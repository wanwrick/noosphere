## Role pillars (one paragraph)

The practice is staffed against three pillars:
1. **Producer-side PO** — owns the contract, the intake, the publish layer.
2. **Platform engineer** — owns the bundle, the rendering, the CI gate.
3. **Governance steward** — owns classification, masking, DPIA, audit trail.

Every engagement assigns at least one named owner per pillar. Without all three, the platform mandate is at risk (`ip/authored/platform-mandate-playbook.md`).
